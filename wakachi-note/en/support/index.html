Wakachi Note is an iPhone app that lets siblings share how their parent who lives apart is doing, along with care and hospital-visit records.

The app is being prepared for release on the App Store. The privacy policy will be published on this site before release.

Features

  • Sign in with Apple or an email address, then invite family members to a shared group.
  • A daily "I'm OK today" check-in, with a notice when no one has checked in by the deadline.
  • Records for notes, medication, meals, health, and hospital visits.

Important

This app is not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent any disease.

It is not a substitute for emergency services, medical devices, or monitoring services. Notifications may not always be delivered. In an emergency, contact the appropriate public emergency service.

Email

The only emails sent by the app are codes triggered by your own action (email address verification, sign-in verification, and password reset). We do not send marketing email. The sender address is no-reply@wakachi-note.app.
