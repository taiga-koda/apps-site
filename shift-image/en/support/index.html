FAQ

Where is my schedule data stored?

Only on your device. Member names and shifts are never sent to our servers, and there is no account or login. Deleting the app deletes the data. There is no device-to-device transfer.

Where do exported images go?

The app opens your device's share sheet. You choose where the image goes (Photos, messaging apps, social apps, etc.). The app does not save images to your photo library by itself.

About ads

A banner ad may appear at the bottom of the preview screen. The one-time "Remove ads" purchase hides it. If you reinstall or change devices, use "Restore purchases" in the app.

Changing ad privacy choices

In regions where it is required, an "Ad privacy settings" button appears on the preview screen. See the Privacy Policy for details.
