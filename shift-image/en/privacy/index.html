Koda Apps ("we") explains below how the mobile app "Shifukawa" (the "App") handles information.

Effective date: November 11, 2026

1. Information stored on your device

Member names, shifts (dates and times), the title, shop name, tagline, and your template and icon choices are stored only on your device. They are not sent to our servers. The App has no accounts or login, and we cannot access this information.

2. Exported images

Exported images are created in a temporary area on your device and handed to the share sheet that you open. Sending an image to another app or service is your own action and we are not involved. The previous temporary image is deleted the next time you export.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App may show a banner ad at the bottom of the preview screen using Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and send information such as:

  • IP address
  • Usage data such as app launches and interactions, and diagnostics (launch time, crashes, performance)
  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID

The App requests non-personalized ads only. It does not track you across other companies' apps and websites for interest-based advertising (and does not show the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt).

In regions where consent is legally required (such as the European Economic Area and the UK), the App asks for your consent through Google's consent management platform (UMP) and does not show ads without it. You can change your choice later from the "Ad privacy settings" button on the preview screen (shown only in regions where it is required).

For how Google handles information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your OS settings.

4. In-app purchase (RevenueCat)

To check and restore the "Remove ads" purchase, the App uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). When you purchase, your purchase history (product, date, etc.) and an anonymous user ID issued by RevenueCat are sent to RevenueCat. Payment is processed by the App Store or Google Play; neither we nor RevenueCat receive your card number or other payment details. We do not collect your name or email address.

5. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect your location, contacts, photo library contents, or camera/microphone data, and uses no analytics tools other than the advertising and purchase services above.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your information to third parties except as required by law. As described in sections 3 and 4, the Google and RevenueCat SDKs collect their respective information.

7. Children

The App is intended mainly for shop and salon operators and is not directed to children under 13.

8. Data deletion

Data on your device is removed when you delete it in the App or delete the App. To request deletion of purchase information held by RevenueCat, contact us below.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when laws or app features change. Important changes will be announced on this page.
